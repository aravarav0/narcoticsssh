What actually won in past years

SIH does not have one overall champion. There is usually one winning team per problem statement (sometimes joint winners). So “winning problems” means: these briefs produced a team the ministry/jury paid.

Honesty first. SIH 2024 has a full official results table. SIH 2025 does not have one public spreadsheet I could download — that section is stitched from nodal-centre press notes and team posts. Smart SNU Hackathon never published a complete winner PDF; campus names come from student / GDG posts. If a line has no PS number, treat it as “this is what they built,” not an official ID.

The pattern (useful for 2026)

Winners are rarely “national satellite GIS with 20 integrations.” They are usually:

  • A phone or web tool a real officer / farmer / student can click in a demo
  • A short, named job (schedule therapy, track a loan, catch a phishing link, book a slot)
  • Software that looks finished, even if the AI is thin
  • Or hardware — but that is a 36-hour / multi-day national finale with kits, not a 12-hour campus night

That is why SIH26231 (photo the colour kit, lock the record) sits in the same family as several past winners: field workflow + evidence, not a science-fair poster.

What this does to your shortlist

Campus SSH rewards a working loop in 12 hours (agri chatbot, bus GPS, waste photo). National SIH rewards a ministry-shaped product (AYUSH scheduler, NCB tracking, loan app, ISL translator). SIH26231 is both: NCB, camera, signed record, demoable overnight. Hardware and Student Innovation do win nationally, but they are a different sport from Friday 10pm at SARC.
