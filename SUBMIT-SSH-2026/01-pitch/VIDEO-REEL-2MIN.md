# SIH26231 — 2-minute demo reel script

**App:** Digital Companion for Field Drug Testing · NCB · Ministry of Home Affairs
**Vibe:** fast, confident, a little cheeky. Think product-launch reel, not a lecture.
**Golden line (say it once, show it once):** *"We're not detecting drugs. We're detecting a colour — fairly — then locking the photo."*
**Runtime target:** ~1:50. Hard cap 2:00.

## Rules that make it not-cringe
- **Assume MUTE.** Reels autoplay silent — every beat must read from on-screen text alone. VO is a bonus.
- **Real screen, real phone.** Screen-record the app + one over-the-shoulder shot of an actual phone photographing the card. No fake mockups.
- **One prop:** the six-square card + a purple "kit" sheet (positive), a white sheet (negative), a random colour (inconclusive). Say "simulated kit — no real narcotics" on screen once.
- **Snappy cuts:** most clips 2–4s. Let the app's animations (Reading colour…, ΔE bars filling) carry the motion.
- Keep the word **PRESUMPTIVE** on screen during any result. It's the honest badge and judges love it.

## Look & sound
- Text: big, bottom-third, high contrast. Brand gold (#E1B653) on dark for captions.
- Music: one upbeat track, ~124 BPM. Drop a beat-hit on each result reveal (positive / negative / inconclusive).
- Transitions: hard cuts on the beat. One "whip" into the corrected-image reveal.

## SHOTLIST (time · visual · on-screen text · VO)

### 0:00–0:05 — COLD OPEN (the problem, instantly)
- **Visual:** Same purple kit shot 3 times side-by-side under 3 lights (daylight / tubelight / yellow lamp) — looks like 3 different colours.
- **On-screen:** `Same test. 3 lights. 3 "answers."`
- **VO:** "A field drug kit works by changing colour — but colour lies under bad light."

### 0:05–0:12 — THE TURN
- **Visual:** Phone lifts, frames card + kit, snaps. App logo/title flashes.
- **On-screen:** `So we gave the kit a fair witness — a phone.`
- **VO:** "We didn't invent a new kit. We gave the existing one a fair witness."

### 0:12–0:22 — CAPTURE (one photo)
- **Visual:** Screen-record: "Get the card and kit in one photo." → tap **Take photo** → **Reading colour…** pulse.
- **On-screen:** `One photo. Card + kit together.`
- **VO:** "One photo. The card and the kit in the same frame."

### 0:22–0:32 — THE FLEX #1: IT FINDS THE CARD ITSELF
- **Visual:** Result photo (the captured-photo block, between "Colour match" and "Why this happened") with the six **gold** boxes on the card squares + the **white KIT (FOUND)** box on the kit. Zoom on the boxes landing.
- **On-screen:** `No lining anything up. It finds all 6 squares by itself.`
- **VO:** "No alignment, no tripod. It finds the card and every square on its own."
- **Note:** "Gold box" = where the app sampled each of the six card squares; the white box = the kit it read.

### 0:32–0:44 — THE FLEX #2: IT UN-LIGHTS THE PHOTO (hero moment)
- **Visual:** Whip transition → on the photo card (right under Colour match) tap the green **Show corrected** button. It starts on "Your photo"; tapping snaps the whole frame from warm/dim to neutral. Tap **Show original** to flip back.
- **On-screen:** `Watch it delete the lighting.` → `Corrected using the card.`
- **VO:** "It reverse-engineers the lighting from the card and re-lights the whole photo. Now the colour is honest."

### 0:44–0:52 — RESULT: POSITIVE
- **Visual:** Hero flips to **POSITIVE**, kit colour **PURPLE · #hex**, "Matches POSITIVE (PURPLE)", ΔE bar slams to full, confidence badge.
- **On-screen:** `PURPLE → POSITIVE` · small tag `PRESUMPTIVE`
- **VO / beat hit:** "Purple. Positive."

### 0:52–1:00 — IT'S NOT A BLACK BOX
- **Visual:** Slow pan over the ΔE bars + "Why this happened" list.
- **On-screen:** `No neural net. Just colour distance you can read.`
- **VO:** "Not a black box, not a guess — it shows the colour distance to each reference."

### 1:00–1:08 — NEGATIVE + INCONCLUSIVE (the honesty combo)
- **Visual:** Quick two-shot: white sheet → **NEGATIVE**; then a random colour → **INCONCLUSIVE** (app refuses).
- **On-screen:** `White → NEGATIVE` … `Not sure → it REFUSES.`
- **VO:** "White reads negative. And when it isn't sure, it refuses — inconclusive is a feature, not a failure."

### 1:08–1:22 — THE SEAL (evidence)
- **Visual:** Tap **Seal this photo** → record: time, GPS, officer ID, SHA-256, ECDSA signature. Then **Flip one byte** → hash jumps. **Verify** → green tick.
- **On-screen:** `Time · GPS · SHA-256 · digital signature` → `Change 1 pixel → hash breaks.`
- **VO:** "Every test is sealed — time, place, officer, a hash and a signature. Tamper with one pixel and the seal breaks."

### 1:22–1:30 — THE LOG
- **Visual:** Log screen, type "purple", results filter instantly.
- **On-screen:** `Searchable, verifiable log.`
- **VO:** "A searchable, verifiable log — the record that field testing was missing."

### 1:30–1:40 — WHY IT'S REAL (feasibility punch)
- **Visual:** Rapid montage: any phone · existing kits · a printed card · no new hardware.
- **On-screen:** `Any phone. Existing kits. A paper card. Zero new hardware.`
- **VO:** "It works today: any phone browser, the kits already in the field, a card that costs almost nothing."

### 1:40–1:50 — CLOSE (golden line + logo)
- **Visual:** Back to camera / clean end card.
- **On-screen:** `We don't detect drugs. We detect colour — fairly — then lock the photo.`
- **VO:** "We're not detecting drugs. We're detecting a colour, fairly — then locking the photo."
- **End card:** `SIH26231 · Digital Companion for Field Drug Testing · NCB · Ministry of Home Affairs · Team [NAME]`
